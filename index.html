WRITING ASSESSMENT

Understand IELTS
Writing Criteria

Task Achievement / Response

How effectively your response addresses the task, presents ideas and fulfils the requirements.

Coherence & Cohesion

How clearly ideas are organised and connected.

Lexical Resource

The range, precision and appropriate use of vocabulary.

Grammatical Range & Accuracy

The variety and accuracy of grammatical structures.

What should you understand?

IELTS Writing is assessed using four criteria. Understanding these criteria helps you identify what your writing needs to improve rather than simply adding complicated vocabulary or grammar.

SPEAKING ASSESSMENT

How IELTS Speaking
Is Assessed

Fluency & Coherence

Ability to speak at an appropriate length and organise ideas coherently.

Lexical Resource

Range and appropriate use of vocabulary.

Grammatical Range & Accuracy

Variety and accuracy of grammatical structures.

Pronunciation

Ability to communicate clearly and be understood.
