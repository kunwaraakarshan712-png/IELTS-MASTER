WRITING & SPEAKING CRITERIA

Both subjective modules are evaluated across 4 equal criteria (25% weight each).

✍️ WRITING ASSESSMENT (25% EACH)

Task Response / Achievement
25% OF WRITING SCORE

Addressing all parts of the prompt, maintaining a clear position throughout, and presenting fully developed ideas.

Coherence & Cohesion
25% OF WRITING SCORE

Logical paragraph arrangement, clear central topic per paragraph, and controlled use of cohesive devices.

Lexical Resource
25% OF WRITING SCORE

Varied vocabulary, natural collocations, precise word choice, and minimal spelling errors.

Grammatical Range & Accuracy
25% OF WRITING SCORE

Mix of complex sentence structures with high proportion of error-free grammar.

🗣️ SPEAKING ASSESSMENT (25% EACH)

Fluency & Coherence
25% OF SPEAKING SCORE

Speaking at length without unnatural pauses, hesitation, or loss of coherence.

Lexical Resource
25% OF SPEAKING SCORE

Using topic-specific phrasing, idiomatic vocabulary naturally, and effective paraphrasing.

Grammatical Range & Accuracy
25% OF SPEAKING SCORE

Accurate use of varied sentence structures (conditionals, clauses) during spontaneous speech.

Pronunciation
25% OF SPEAKING SCORE

Clear articulation, correct word stress, intonation flow, and easy intelligibility.
